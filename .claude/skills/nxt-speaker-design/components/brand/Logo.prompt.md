One-line: the brand badge — light ground gets `navy`, navy ground gets `white`; copy `assets/logo-badge-*` next to your page.

```jsx
<Logo variant="navy" size={44} assetBase="../../assets" />
<Logo variant="white" size={36} orientation="horizontal" assetBase="../../assets" />
```

Rules: minimum 28px; clear space = 25% of badge diameter; never stretch, recolour or add effects; the badge may be used oversized at ≤15% opacity as a watermark on navy bands.
